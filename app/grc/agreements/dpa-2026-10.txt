CLOUDSHIELD DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (TEMPLATE, VERSION 2026-10)

STATUS: Template prepared for an academic capstone. It has not been reviewed
by legal counsel and is not legal advice. Do not rely on it for production
personal data without review.

1. Parties and roles
1.1 The customer ("Controller") is the tenant organisation named by its
    CloudShield tenant identifier. The operator of the CloudShield platform
    ("Processor") processes documents and their metadata on the Controller's
    behalf.
1.2 This agreement is intended to address GDPR Article 28(3) and is concluded
    in electronic form (GDPR Article 28(9)). Equivalent obligations under
    Egypt Law No. 151 of 2020 and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law are
    addressed to the extent mapped in the CloudShield GRC hub (items marked
    "verify" there are not established).

2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose
2.1 Subject matter: storage, retrieval, deletion and audit of documents the
    Controller uploads.
2.2 Duration: from acceptance until the tenant is closed, plus the erasure
    period in clause 9.
2.3 Categories of data subjects and personal data: as decided by the
    Controller. The Controller classifies each document (public, internal,
    confidential, restricted).

3. Instructions
3.1 The Processor processes personal data only on the Controller's
    documented instructions, which are the Controller's use of the CloudShield
    API and portal, unless required by applicable law.

4. Confidentiality
4.1 Persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by
    confidentiality. Platform security staff (the SecOps role) have no access
    to tenant documents.

5. Security (GDPR Article 32)
5.1 Encryption at rest with customer-managed KMS keys; TLS from the browser
    to the edge and to the API; mandatory TOTP multi-factor authentication;
    a web application firewall at the edge; automated containment playbooks;
    an insert-only audit trail (conditional writes, no update or delete
    permission). The last hop inside the platform network is plain HTTP over
    AWS PrivateLink.
5.2 Tenant isolation is enforced by the application and the data partition
    key; it is not cryptographic.

6. Sub-processors
6.1 Amazon Web Services, Inc. (hosting, region us-east-1). The Controller
    authorises this sub-processor by accepting this agreement.

7. Data subject rights
7.1 The Processor provides document listing, download and deletion through
    the API so the Controller can answer requests.

8. Personal data breaches
8.1 The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay after becoming
    aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data, with the
    incident report produced by the platform.

9. Deletion and return
9.1 Deleting a document removes its metadata at once and places a delete
    marker on its stored object; earlier object versions expire within 30
    days. Point-in-time backups of metadata expire within 35 days.

10. Audits
10.1 The Controller's administrators and auditors can read the tenant audit
     trail at any time.

11. International transfers
11.1 Data is hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-1). Transfers rely on
     safeguards to be agreed (for example standard contractual clauses under
     Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) or an adequacy decision
     where it applies.

12. Acceptance
12.1 Acceptance is recorded as a click-through by an MFA-authenticated tenant
     administrator who confirms authority to bind the tenant. The record
     holds the text's SHA-256, the time, the user identifier, the source IP
     and the request identifier. This is evidence of acceptance, not a
     qualified or advanced electronic signature.
