CLOUDSHIELD SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (TEMPLATE, VERSION 2026-10)

STATUS: Template prepared for an academic capstone. It has not been reviewed
by legal counsel and is not legal advice. The platform runs in a lab account;
no commercial service is offered.

1. Service
1.1 The CloudShield document API and portal, served through Amazon
    CloudFront and Amazon API Gateway in AWS us-east-1.

2. Availability objective
2.1 Objective: 99.5% monthly availability of the API, measured as successful
    responses to the health endpoint, excluding scheduled maintenance
    announced 48 hours ahead.
2.2 This is an objective, not a guarantee. No service credits apply.

3. Security incident handling
3.1 Automated containment playbooks target a mean time to contain of 60
    seconds for the incident types they cover. Incidents outside their scope
    are routed to an analyst.
3.2 The Controller is told of an incident affecting its data without undue
    delay (see the Data Processing Agreement, clause 8).

4. Support
4.1 Best effort during the capstone's working hours. Severity is assessed by
    the platform team.

5. Data durability and recovery
5.1 Document metadata has point-in-time recovery (35 days). Stored objects
    are versioned; previous versions are kept for 30 days.

6. Exclusions
6.1 Outages caused by AWS, by the Controller's own configuration, or by
    traffic that the web application firewall blocks.

7. Acceptance
7.1 Recorded as a click-through by an MFA-authenticated tenant administrator.
    This is evidence of acceptance, not a qualified or advanced electronic
    signature.
